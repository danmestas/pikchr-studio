Canvas gestures & shortcuts

Drag empty canvas to pan. Two fingers pan and pinch to zoom. Mouse wheel/trackpad scroll pans; Ctrl/⌘ + scroll zooms at the pointer. Hold Space or use the middle mouse button to pan over shapes.

Ctrl/⌘ Z: Undo · Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z (or Ctrl Y): Redo · Ctrl/⌘ D: Duplicate · Ctrl/⌘ A: Select movable shapes · Delete: Remove selection · Escape: Cancel drag / clear selection.

When the canvas is focused: + / − zoom · 0 / 1 fit diagram · F focus selection · Arrow keys move 0.1 inch, Shift moves 1 inch, Alt moves 0.01 inch. Text fields keep their normal shortcuts. Navigation does not change your source or undo history.

Drag a shape to preview ranked source edits. Accepted changes queue here; Apply all commits them, Discard all restores the original.
