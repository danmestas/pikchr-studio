Canvas-first mockup

Static sketch for direction only. Hover a shape to see its attach points. Toggle the source drawer with the </> button.

  1. One floating tool palette. Shapes and connectors are one click away with single-key shortcuts. It moves to the bottom on phones.
  2. Contextual toolbar on the selection. Label, fill, stroke, size, connect, duplicate, delete. Replaces the inspector and the two rows under the canvas.
  3. Drag-to-connect. Hovering any shape shows four attach dots. Drag from a dot and a live arrow follows the pointer, the target shape and its nearest anchor light up, and the badge names the edit. Dropping on empty space creates a connected shape there.
  4. Candidates next to the change. The ranked source edit appears beside the object, not in a side panel. Enter accepts, brackets cycle, Escape keeps the original.
  5. Source is a drawer, not a pane. Selection highlights the matching lines. Typing re-renders live.
  6. One status chip. Rendered / invalid / queued count, with Apply. The canvas never locks; it keeps working on the last valid render.

Kept off-canvas behind ☰ or ⋯: documents, backup, components, export options, advanced tools.
